Minecraft.

A 5-backend Velocity network running since 2024 — Survival SMP, Anarchy, Minigames, Limbo, and a Lobby. Players: friends, family, and whoever finds the public address. All connected to a Telegram bot for moderation and status.

Network
Proxy

Velocity 4.1, Geyser for Bedrock clients, AuthMe proxy auth, ClientCatcher to fingerprint joiners. Public on :25565 (Java) and :25566 (Bedrock).

Backends

Lobby (auth hub) · Survival (Paper 1.21, 7 GB heap, Aikar G1GC) · Anarchy (Jobs, Crates, Duels, Skyblock) · Minigames (Bedwars + MinigamesCore) · Limbo (PicoLimbo, loopback).

Telegram

tg-mc-bot: /link, /reset, /who, online alerts, watchdog notifications. Admin-only commands gated by tg user ID.

Plugins — Survival SMP
EssentialsX

/home, /sethome, /tpa, /spawn. The minimum needed for a multiplayer server.

GriefPrevention

Claim blocks, no admin intervention. Players police themselves.

BlueMap

Live web map at :8100, renders once a week. The only way to find your base after 3 months.

Plugins — Anarchy
Economy

Jobs (grind money) · Vault (the bank) · AuctionHousePlus (player-to-player) · CrazyCrates (loot boxes).

Combat

CustomEnchants · Duels · IridiumSkyblock (private islands) · MythicMobs (boss spawns every 2h).

World

Multiverse (extra worlds) · ChunkyBorder (render boundary) · GriefPrevention (yes, even on anarchy) · CustomEnchants · LuckPerms.

Operations
Watchdog

Cron every 2 min. If any backend port is dead, restart the screen. Logs to a file. Sometimes TG-notifies me.

Backups

Daily tar of every world, kept 7 days, 4 weeks, 6 months. Restore-tested on Sundays.

Performance

Survival: view-distance 8, sim-distance 6 (was 32, now stable at 20 TPS). Chunky pregen r=3000 once, then never again.
