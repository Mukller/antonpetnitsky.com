Robotics & Competitions

Robots I designed, printed, wired and debugged myself — then took to Robofinist and FIRA competitions.

Arkanoid Robot "Robo Arkasha"
Robofinist 2026

Autonomous robot that plays Arkanoid in real time: a PC-side OpenCV tracker follows the red ball (HSV calibration), sends commands over UDP to the ESP32, which drives the paddle via a BLDC motor on an ESC plus L298M side motors. The whole chassis is 3D-printed from PETG.

VisionOpenCV, HSV calibration, UDP stream → PC
MCUESP32 (Wi-Fi UDP client)
ActuatorsBLDC + ESC, 2× L298M side motors
PowerLiPo 3S
ChassisPETG, 3D print
ESP32 OpenCV UDP BLDC + ESC PETG chassis LiPo 3S
Self-Balancing Robot
Robofinist 2026 · with teammate Kostya

Two-wheeled inverted pendulum. The ESP32 fuses MPU6050 IMU data and runs a PID loop I tuned from scratch, driving two NEMA17 steppers through TMC2209 drivers. Several months of assembly and debugging with my teammate Kostya.

IMUMPU6050
MCUESP32
Motors2× NEMA17 + TMC2209 (stealthChop)
ControlPID, tuned from scratch

ESP32 TMC2209 MPU6050 PID NEMA17
